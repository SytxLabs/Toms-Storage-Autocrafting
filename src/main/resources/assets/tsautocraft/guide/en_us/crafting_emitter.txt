Crafting Emitter

Gives a redstone signal depending on one item of the network:
- below: while the stock is below the amount,
- above: while the stock is at least the amount,
- craft: the item counts as craftable and the signal is on while a job needs it (e.g. to switch on a farm). The items must come back through an Importer or into storage.

The full block emits on all sides. The directional (and framed directional) variant only emits out of its front.
